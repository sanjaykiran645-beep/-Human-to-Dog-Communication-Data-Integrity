# Engine B Experimental Field Trial Protocol
## Establishing Learned, Intention-Specific Responses to Canine-Inspired Acoustic Cues

**Protocol ID:** CSAI-PROTO-ENG-B-001  
**Version:** 2.0.0  
**Principal Investigators:** Senior Canine Bioacoustics Researcher, Ethology & Animal Behaviour Lead, Veterinary Safety Reviewer  
**Ethical Approval:** Non-invasive positive reinforcement field trial guidelines (ARRIVE guidelines compliant)  
**Date:** 2026-11-15  

---

## 1. Scientific Objective & Scope

The central hypothesis of Engine B is that **synthesized and reference-derived canine-inspired acoustic cues can serve as discriminable, learnable conditioned stimuli producing reproducible, intention-specific behavioural responses in domestic dogs (*Canis lupus familiaris*)**.

This protocol defines the scientific methodology required to establish whether dogs:
1. Discriminate Engine B acoustic cues from generic tones, ambient noise, and ordinary commands.
2. Form reliable conditioned associations between specific acoustic cues and desired behavioural actions.
3. Retain these associations across intervals and transfer them across environments.

**Non-Negotiable Premise:**  
Canine-inspired cues are **not** assumed to have innate semantic meaning. True communication is demonstrated only through controlled, observable, reproducible stimulus-response contingency.

---

## 2. Experimental Design & Trial Structure

### 2.1 Participant Selection & Consenting
- **Target Cohort:** Minimum $N = 30$ privately owned domestic dogs across varied breeds, body sizes, and training backgrounds.
- **Inclusion Criteria:** Clinically healthy dogs aged 1–10 years; normal hearing confirmed via startle-audiometry screening; signed informed owner consent.
- **Exclusion Criteria:** History of noise phobia, severe separation anxiety, generalized fearfulness, or aggressive escalation to acoustic stimuli.

### 2.2 Trial Sequence & Timing
Each trial follows a strict 3-phase temporal sequence:
1. **Pre-Cue Baseline Period (10 seconds):**
   - Dog is unrestrained in a low-distraction environment.
   - Continuous multi-angle video recording captures resting posture, orientation, and baseline activity.
2. **Acoustic Cue Delivery (0.2–2.5 seconds):**
   - Stimulus delivered via a calibrated flat-response loudspeaker positioned 2.0 meters from the dog at dog ear level.
   - Calibrated SPL: 60–68 dBA at 1 meter (strictly capped at 75 dBA).
   - Zero human movement, eye contact, or verbal cues during playback.
3. **Post-Cue Observation Window (15 seconds):**
   - High-speed video recording (60+ fps) tracks orienting latency, motor action latency, path trajectory, and subsequent behavioural state.

---

## 3. Blind Video Review & Inter-Observer Agreement

To eliminate owner and experimenter bias:
- All video clips are trimmed to identical 25-second windows (10s pre, 15s post).
- Audio tracks are digitally muted during video scoring so reviewers cannot hear whether a target cue, control sound, or silence was played.
- Two independent certified animal behaviour reviewers score each trial using the standardized ethogram:
  - Latency to head turn toward loudspeaker ($t_{\text{orient}}$).
  - Latency to initiate motor action ($t_{\text{action}}$).
  - Specific action executed (e.g. approach speaker, approach owner, sit/down, play bow, retreat, freeze).
  - Presence of conflict/stress indicators.
- Inter-observer reliability must achieve Cohen's kappa $\kappa \ge 0.80$ before trial data is admitted to the validation corpus.

---

## 4. Controlled Progression Pathway

```
Stage 1: Acoustic Discrimination (Differential Orienting vs Controls)
   │
   ▼
Stage 2: Operant Conditioning (Pairing Cue + Primary Reinforcer)
   │
   ▼
Stage 3: Cue Discrimination (Contrasting 2 Engine B Cues: e.g. Come vs Stay)
   │
   ▼
Stage 4: Extinction Resistance & Retention (7-day / 30-day testing)
   │
   ▼
Stage 5: Generalization Across Novel Contexts (Outdoor / Distraction)
```

No claim of "two-way canine communication" may be published or released to end-users until a cue achieves $\ge 75\%$ intention congruence across Stage 3 and Stage 4.
